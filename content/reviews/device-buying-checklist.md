Write down the tasks the device must handle and decide what you can spend in total. Include essential accessories and the cost of a battery replacement if you’re considering a used device.

## Smartphones

Check network and SIM support for the exact model number, especially for an import. Confirm how long it will receive security updates and whether the storage can hold your apps, photos and offline media. For a used phone, test the microphone, speakers, cameras and charging port. Ask the seller to remove their account and confirm you can set up the device after a reset.

## Laptops and computers

List the software you need for your course or work. Check its operating system and hardware requirements, then check how much memory and storage the exact laptop configuration has. For programming, account for the tools you’ll run together, especially local containers or virtual machines. When buying refurbished, test the keyboard, trackpad, display, webcam and the ports you use. Ask for a battery health report and confirm whether memory and storage can be replaced.

## PC hardware

Start with the workload and the parts you plan to keep. Check the motherboard’s processor support list and required firmware version, then match the memory type. Check that the graphics card fits the case and that the power supply offers the required connectors. Include cooling, shipping and any needed upgrades when you compare costs.

## Wearables

Confirm that the companion app supports your phone. Check which features need subscriptions, a phone connection or regional support. Compare charging routines and whether you can get a replacement strap or charger. Use health features to follow general trends unless the manufacturer explicitly documents a medical use for the exact feature.

## Headphones and audio

Prioritise the setting you’ll use them in. For calls, try the microphone in an environment with similar background noise. For commuting, check the fit and how securely the headphones sit when moving. Check wired and wireless compatibility with your actual devices and see whether the cushions or ear tips are replaceable.

## Accessories

For a USB-C hub, confirm which port carries power, video or data on both the hub and the device. Check the monitor resolution and refresh rate supported by the full connection. For a charger, match the device’s charging standard and check the power rating of the cable. A shared connector shape tells you little about the supported features.

## Warranty and returns in Nigeria

Ask who provides the warranty, where to take the device for service, and whether the terms cover imported or refurbished units. Get the exact model, condition, return window and repair terms in writing. Keep the invoice and check the serial number or device identifier against it before accepting delivery.
