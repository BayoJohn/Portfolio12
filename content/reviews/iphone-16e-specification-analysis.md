This analysis uses Apple’s published specifications. StackedByBayo did not test either phone.

## Display and processor

Both phones use a 6.1-inch OLED display and an A18 chip. Apple specifies a higher typical maximum display brightness for the iPhone 16: 1,000 nits versus 800 nits for the 16e. The iPhone 16 has a five-core GPU and the 16e has a four-core GPU. That difference alone cannot tell you the frame rate to expect in a particular game.

## Rear cameras

The 16e gets its 2× framing from its main camera sensor. The iPhone 16 adds a separate Ultra Wide camera, which can help you fit more of a room or a group into the frame when you cannot step back. Apple’s specifications also list macro photography for the iPhone 16. Inspect sample photos from the model you’re considering if image quality is a deciding factor.

## Charging and accessories

Both phones charge over USB-C. Apple lists standard Qi wireless charging for the 16e and MagSafe and Qi2 for the iPhone 16. If you already use a magnetic car mount, wallet or charger, check exactly what you need from that accessory before choosing.

## What to check before buying

If you’re buying a used or imported unit in Nigeria, get the model number and confirm SIM support with your carrier. Ask the seller to show battery health and verify that the phone can be set up after a reset. Get the warranty and return terms in writing, including who handles a repair locally.
