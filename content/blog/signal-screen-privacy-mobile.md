Signal announced new mobile screen protections on November 20. The changes address what happens after a message is visible on a device. An on-device assistant that can read the screen sees a different surface from the encrypted connection that delivered the message.

## What the settings do

On supported iPhones, Signal says the protection is enabled by default with Signal 8.37 or later on iOS 27 or later. Its support page lists Signal 8.35.1 or later on Android, where screen security can activate when the app detects an assistant trying to read the screen. Android versions and vendors vary, so check the control on your own device. On both platforms, the settings can affect your ability to take a screenshot inside the app.

## Where the limits matter

Signal says it cannot reliably apply equivalent screenshot blocking on macOS. Its announcement recommends adding Signal to Siri’s Excluded Apps on macOS 27 where that control is available. The mobile changes also leave the message recipient’s device outside your control. For sensitive conversations, think about who can see or capture the contents at either end as well as how the message travels between them.
