Huawei scheduled the Mate X8’s China launch for November 24 and published specifications for the new foldable. The standard model puts a 6.54-inch cover screen on the outside and an 8.2-inch display inside. It is a different answer to the same pocket-versus-workspace question posed by Apple’s smaller iPhone Duo.

## The display spec worth comparing

Huawei lists an adaptive refresh rate of 1–120 Hz on both OLED screens. The standard Mate X8’s outer screen is 2444 × 1132 pixels, while its 2424 × 2340 inner screen is much closer to square. That shape can be useful for two columns of text or two apps. Video framed for a wide screen can leave large unused areas. Buyers who mostly watch video may gain less usable area than the diagonal suggests.

The company lists a typical 6,200 mAh capacity and gives a lower rated figure of 6,025 mAh. Capacity describes how much charge the battery holds. Runtime depends on the workload and which screen is active. Neither of those catalogue figures predicts how long a day of navigation, video and split-screen use will last.

## The import question

Huawei’s China specification lists HarmonyOS 7.0. Before importing, check the software and services you rely on, the model’s network compatibility and who pays if the hinge or inner screen needs repair. These questions can decide whether the device works as your main phone. Huawei’s China launch and specifications establish what it has announced for that market. They do not establish Nigerian pricing or availability.
