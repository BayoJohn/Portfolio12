About StackedByBayo

$tagline

StackedByBayo is an independent publication covering the technology shaping everyday life, from consumer electronics and software to artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and technology education.

Why We Exist

Keeping up with technology can feel like a job of its own. Understanding why a chip shortage changes the price of a laptop, or why an app update matters to your privacy, should be possible without a technical background. We explain the details that make a difference and give curious readers room to go deeper.

Our Editorial Philosophy

We ask what happened, why it happened, and what readers can do with that information. We would rather wait for evidence than publish an appealing guess. Our work aims to explain trade-offs clearly, show where the evidence comes from, and leave room for readers to reach their own conclusions.

Read our editorial standards

Our Perspective

Technology is global. Its costs and trade-offs are local. Where relevant, we consider the experiences of readers in Nigeria and elsewhere in Africa, including affordability, local availability, repair and warranty options, and the cost of staying connected. We identify the market we are discussing when conditions vary by country.
