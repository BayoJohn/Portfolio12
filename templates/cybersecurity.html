$essentials

Malware

Malware is software designed to harm a system or misuse its access.

Ransomware
Blocks access to files or systems and demands payment. Some incidents also involve stolen data.
Trojans
Masquerade as legitimate programs or files to persuade someone to run them.
Spyware
Collects information such as activity or sensitive data without informed consent.
$malware_stories

Threats

Some attacks exploit trust in a message, supplier or service.

Phishing
Messages or sites impersonate trusted people or services to obtain information or prompt an unsafe action.
Supply-chain attacks
A compromise at a supplier, software dependency or service provider can affect the people and systems that rely on it.
