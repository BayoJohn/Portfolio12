Report a factual error

Use the corrections email link. Include the article URL, the sentence or claim at issue, and what you believe is wrong. A link to reliable evidence is helpful if you have one. Leave out passwords, account details and confidential documents.

How material corrections are recorded

When a factual error is confirmed, our policy is to fix the article and leave a dated note describing what changed. This includes errors about who said something, how a figure was calculated, which versions are affected, or any other detail that changes the meaning of the story. Article pages can display a dated correction alongside their existing publication and update information.

Updates and minor edits

New developments may warrant an update or follow-up article. An update should make clear when it adds information that became available after publication. Spelling, punctuation and presentation edits can be made without a correction note when they leave the meaning intact.
