Editorial independence

Our standard is to base coverage and recommendations on evidence and relevance to readers. We aim to identify a relevant personal or commercial relationship alongside the affected story.

Links and commercial arrangements

Links may point to manufacturers, official downloads, product documentation or outside publishers. When a link could earn the publication a commission, our policy is to identify that relationship near the link. Any paid placement or sponsored article should carry a clear label before the reader enters the story. The Shop page is a placeholder.

How a product was evaluated

Look for the evaluation note in a review or product overview. We aim to identify when a product was loaned or supplied, and to distinguish our own observations from published specifications, manufacturer statements and other sources.
