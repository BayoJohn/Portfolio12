Visiting and searching the site

The site is configured for Vercel hosting. Serving a page involves processing request information, which can include an IP address, browser information and the requested URL. The site’s search filters the available results in your browser. Your search term appears in the URL, so it may be present in browser history and hosting logs. Avoid entering private information in search.

Preferences and editorial sign-in

The public page templates do not include a reader analytics script. Hosting settings can separately affect what request data is recorded.

Light or dark mode is saved in your browser’s local storage and can be cleared through its site settings. You can read the public site without a reader account. The separate editorial sign-in uses Supabase for authentication and a session cookie when an administrator signs in. Supabase also provides the editorial database and image storage configured by the site.

Fonts, images and external links

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Some articles load images from Unsplash. When your browser requests these resources, their providers receive the information needed to serve them, which can include your IP address. External sources, publishers and social profiles have their own data practices when you visit them.

See Google Fonts’ FAQ and Unsplash’s privacy policy.

Email and privacy questions

When you email StackedByBayo, your address, message and any attachments are handled by the email services involved in delivering and receiving them. We use the information you send to understand and handle your enquiry. Contact links open your email app and leave sending the message to you.

For questions about your information, including retention or a request to remove something you sent, contact StackedByBayo. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in ordinary email.
